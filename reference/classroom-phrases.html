5.Two spellings to remember

  • こんにちは and こんばんは end in は, pronounced wa. A survival from pre-war spelling. It happens in these greetings and in the grammatical particle は — nowhere else worth worrying about yet.
  • ちょっと uses a small ょ. A full-size よ would read chiyotto. Small kana shrink the preceding sound instead of adding a syllable; the small っ likewise doubles the following consonant, which is why it is matte and not mate.
