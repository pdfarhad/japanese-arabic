1.How to read this page

Every line carries two buttons and both start closed. That is the whole design: the value of a reading page is the moment you try the line yourself, before anything helps you.

ButtonWhat it doesUse it when
🔊 Reads the line aloud in Japanese After you have tried to read it. Hearing it first turns the page into listening practice, which is a different exercise.
EN·AR Opens the English, the Arabic with its transliteration, and a note where the line has one After you have guessed. Press it again to close the line back up.
🔊 inside the reveal Reads the Arabic aloud Every time — the Arabic is a second vocabulary here, not a gloss.

The furigana is the book's, including where it is incomplete

Minna no Nihongo annotates characters, not spoken words, so 地下1階 is printed with ちか over the first two characters and かい over the last — but the line is said ちかいっかい, because いち and かい collide. The play buttons say the whole line correctly. Seven lines carry a numeral the furigana cannot voice — the two room and price lines, the two handwritten digits, 9歳, 408 and this one — and each says in its note how it is actually read.
