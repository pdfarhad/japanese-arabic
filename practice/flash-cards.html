Practice · ふくしゅう — chapters 10 to 14

Flash Cards

A practice deck, not a lesson. Nothing here is new — every word was taught, glossed and checked in its own chapter. This page exists to stop them slipping away again.

216 words · five chapters · 30 a session, dealt fresh every time you load the page · it remembers what you miss, in this browser only

1.How to use it

The card shows one side. You retrieve the rest before you turn it — that beat, where nothing on screen is helping you, is the entire exercise. Then you tell it the truth.

ControlWhat it does
Front — Mixed · かな · English · العربية Which side you are asked from. The back always shows all three. Mixed is the default and the one to use: it picks a different side per card, so you cannot settle into one direction.
Chapters Which chapters are in play. All five on is the point of the page — the words were learned in blocks and have to survive being shuffled.
Fresh session Thirty cards drawn from everything in scope, weighted so the ones you keep missing come round more often.
Trouble words Only the words currently catching you out, hardest first. Greyed until there are some.
space Turns the card. 1 is Knew it, 2 is Missed it — and neither key does anything until the card is turned, so a stray press cannot cost you a word.
🔊 Says the Japanese, or the Arabic. The card speaks itself the moment you turn it — never before, or hearing it would be the answer.

Be honest with Missed it

Half-remembering counts as missing. The deck is only as useful as the record you give it, and the cost of an honest miss is that the word comes back — which is what you want. The cost of a generous Knew it is that it does not.

The deck needs JavaScript. Every word in it is listed in lesson 10, lesson 11, lesson 12, lesson 13 and lesson 14, with its full card and its note.

3.What it remembers, and where

Each card carries a wrong-score. Missing it adds one, up to five. Knowing it takes one off, down to zero. That score is all the weighting runs on:

CardHow often it comes up
Never seen2.5×
Seen, score 01× — the baseline
Score 13×
Score 37×
Score 5 — the ceiling11×

The decay is the important half. Without it, a word you fumbled once in July would still be crowding the deck in December, and the trouble list would slowly become the whole course. Getting it right twice gets it out.

Your lifetime counts — how many times known, how many missed — never decay. They are shown on the back of a card you have met before, and they break ties in the trouble list.

Where this is stored

In this browser, in localStorage, under one key. It is never sent anywhere — there is no account, no server and no sync, which is also why it does not follow you to your phone. Clearing your browser data clears it, and Forget my history at the end of a session clears it deliberately. If storage is blocked — Safari private browsing does this — the deck still works and simply stops remembering.

4.Where these words come from

Five chapters, kept as five separate sets rather than poured into one. A set whose premise is “the words of chapter 12” would stop being true the moment chapter 13 was appended to it.

Lesson 13 41 Places, floors and prices

A word that appears in two chapters is dealt once, from the earlier one, so a session never asks you the same thing twice.
